Subtitles that never mishear.

Automatic subtitles are built from what a machine thinks it heard. The word that slips is usually the one that matters most to you: the brand name, the product name, the technical term. Ours start from the script, not from the machine’s ears. What appears on screen is exactly what was approved.

Frequently asked questions

Are the subtitles from automatic transcription?

No. The text comes straight from the script you approved, word for word. The machine is used only to time each word, so brand names and technical terms are never misheard.

How long does one video take?

A first draft usually takes days after the script is approved, not weeks. What slows projects down is decision ping-pong, which is why there are two approval points before production starts: script and storyboard.

Can faces and style stay consistent across many videos?

That is the core of this service. Your brand’s faces, locations, props, colors, and voice are locked once at the start, then reused across videos and across months.

Won’t AI video look cheap?

Not when it’s directed. What makes AI video look cheap is rarely the model. It’s the absence of decisions: characters whose faces drift, lighting that doesn’t match, a generic script. People make those decisions at the script and storyboard stages, and you approve them before production.

Who owns the finished work?

You do. The brand world we build for you and all final files are yours.

Which languages for voice-over and subtitles?

English and Indonesian are handled in the Studio. For other language pairs, we work with Translexi, our parent company, which has done translation since 2009.

Does the video need better words?

Scripts, captions, and the campaign copy that goes with your video are written by our copywriting team, in English and Indonesian.
