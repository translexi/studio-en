AI video production:
one world, dozens of stories.

AI video production for brands: ads and brand video, monthly social content, strategy built on account numbers, plus subtitles and voice-over. Ten separate videos usually look like ten different vendors. Ten videos from one world look like a brand that knows what it wants. We set the world first — who appears, where, with what props, in what colours and voice — and every piece of content grows out of that.

Four desks in this division

01

Ads & brand video

Short ads, talking heads, product videos, and corporate videos. Formats 16:9 · 9:16 · 1:1 · 4:5, from 15 seconds to long-form.

02

Monthly social content

Twenty pieces a month that look like family. Your faces, locations, props, colours, and voice get locked, then reused across months — month nine's content is still family with month one's. Run as a monthly retainer, not a one-off project.

03

Strategy & analytics

We don't guess. We count first, then guess politely: your account's numbers get read first, the brief comes from those findings, and this month's results correct next month's plan. Account audits, topic research, editorial calendars, monthly reports.

04

Subtitles & voice-over

Most automatic subtitles are built from what a machine thinks it heard, so a word slips now and then — and the word that slips is usually your brand name. Ours don't work that way: the text comes from the script, word for word, and the machine is used only to decide when each word appears. What is written is exactly what was approved. The voice-over is an AI voice we pick and direct until it sounds natural — no talent schedules, no re-recording fee when the script changes. You get SRT/VTT, burned-in subtitles, and a finished audio track.

Frequently asked

Are the subtitles from automatic transcription?

No. The text comes straight from the script you approved, word for word; the machine is used only to decide when each word appears. So no brand name or technical term gets misheard — what shows on screen is exactly what is written in the script.

How long does one video take?

The first draft is usually a matter of days after the script is approved, not weeks. What actually causes delays is rarely production — it's decision ping-pong, which is why there are two approval gates (script and storyboard) before production starts.

Can faces and style stay consistent across many videos?

That's precisely what we sell. Your brand world — faces, locations, props, colours, voice — gets locked once at the start, then reused across videos and across months.

Who owns the finished work?

You do. The brand world we build for you is yours, and so are all the final files.

Which languages for voice-over and subtitles?

Indonesian and English are the main lanes at the Studio. For other pairs, we just walk to the next desk: Translexi, our parent, whose whole business has been exactly that since 2009.

What formats and lengths?

16:9, 9:16, 1:1, and 4:5 — from 15 seconds to long-form. One piece can be born in several formats at once, because making variants is precisely the work we hand to the machine.

Need something that isn't video?

Document, app, and website localization are our parent’s desks — handled by the same people who’ve spent years guarding language for brands that can’t afford to say the wrong thing.
