How to start

  1. Send a video brief, or send one webtoon episode.
  2. We reply with direction and an estimate — a formal quote follows if it fits.
  3. Agreed? Video starts from the script. Webtoon starts from the source files.

What actually helps in a brief

For video: who the audience is, what they should do after watching, where it runs, and how many pieces a month you need. If you already have an account running, say so — we read the numbers first. For webtoon: the title, source and target languages, episode count, and what file formats you hold. If the publisher gave you a style guide or a glossary, that saves the most time. Don't have all of it? Send what you have and we'll ask for the rest. Examples of work you've used before help too, including the ones you think failed: one rejected example teaches us your taste faster than ten sentences of explanation.

Response time

We work from Yogyakarta (GMT+7). Briefs that arrive during working hours are usually answered the same day, and within one working day at the latest. For unreleased material, an NDA can be signed before the first file moves.
