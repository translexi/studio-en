About us

Why's a translation house
in the picture business?

For more than fifteen years, our job has really been just one thing: making sure meaning survives the move from one container to another. From language to language, from one market to another, from how one person speaks to how another person hears.

Video and webtoon aren't a leap. They're just the next container. Scripts, voice-over, subtitles, lettering, localization — all fields we already worked in every day, long before we owned a camera.

What changes is only the shape of the output. What doesn't change: someone is still responsible for making sure your meaning arrives intact.

About AI

The AI works hard. The one who signs off is still human.

What the machine does

In video production: repetitive work — variants, sizes, language versions, and drafts that are meant to be thrown away. The machine doesn't get tired, and that's exactly what it's for.
