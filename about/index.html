Why a language house opened a creative studio.

Translexi Studio is the creative studio of Translexi. Translexi has worked in translation and localization since 2009. The Studio handles what comes after translation: pictures, copy, and comics.

For seventeen years, Translexi has really done one job: making sure meaning survives the move to a new container. From one language to another, from one market to another, from the way one person speaks to the way another person hears.

Scripts, voice-over, subtitles, and lettering were daily work for us long before we produced video. When the same clients began asking for more than text, we formed the Studio: a creative team with a language house’s discipline.

What changed is the shape of the output. What didn’t: someone is always accountable for making sure your meaning arrives intact.

One company, three brands.

Three brands under PT Solusi Bahasa Internasional, each with its own team. We send you next door when your brief belongs there.

Where AI fits in our work

The machine handles repetition: variants, sizes, format versions, and visual drafts. Story decisions, fact-checking, final approval, and the copy that carries the brand stay with people. All webtoon work is translated and lettered by people.
