Where AI fits in our work

What the machine does

In video production: repetitive work such as variants, sizes, format versions, and visual drafts meant to be thrown away. The machine doesn’t get tired, which is exactly what it’s for.

What stays with people

Story decisions, fact-checking, and final approval. Copy that carries the brand is written and edited by people; AI is used only for research, checking, and variants. All webtoon work is translated and lettered by people.

What you own

The brand world we build, the copy, the glossaries, and every final file. Plus one name who is accountable if something goes wrong.
